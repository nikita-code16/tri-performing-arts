GABHA

School of Semi-Classical & Bollywood Dance

About GABHA

GABHA is a dance learning space that combines semi-classical grace with the energy and creativity of Bollywood dance.

Our Approach

GABHA focuses on practical dance learning through regular practice, rhythm, movement and performance.

Semi-Classical Dance

Students learn graceful movements, expressions, rhythm and basic classical elements in a simple and engaging way.

Bollywood Dance

Bollywood dance brings energetic choreography, expressions, coordination and fun into the learning experience.

Confidence & Expression

Dance helps students build confidence, improve stage presence and express themselves comfortably through movement.

Learning Experience

GABHA encourages discipline, creativity and regular practice while creating a positive environment for students.
