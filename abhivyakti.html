Abhivyakti School of Film & Theatre

Abhivyakti School of Film & Theatre is a theatre education program in Gadhinglaj.

About Us

About Abhivyakti

Abhivyakti School of Film & Theatre is a theatre education initiative in Gadhinglaj focused on practical learning, creativity and performance.

Our Faculty

Our faculty members guide students through practical theatre exercises, acting training, voice and speech development and stage performance.

Types of Courses

Foundation in Acting

A foundation-level course focusing on basic acting techniques, expressions, character development and stage confidence.

Advanced Theatre and Acting

Advanced practical training in theatre and acting, including performance techniques, character work and stage activities.

Voice and Speech Training

Training focused on voice modulation, pronunciation, speech clarity, dialogue delivery and effective communication.
